POWER HIT RULE FIX

The arena now resolves each round as a central power collision:
- Both selected powers launch toward the centre at the same time.
- The power with the higher strength breaks through the collision.
- Only that power continues to the opponent and causes damage.
- The fighter who owns the weaker power is the only fighter who reacts as hurt / falls and loses health.
- The fighter whose power breaks through remains in guard/attack recovery and does not play a hurt reaction.
- If both powers have equal strength, one is randomly selected to break through so only one fighter takes a hit.
- A shield reduces damage to its owner if that owner is the one hit; a healing power restores health to its owner only if that owner's power wins the clash.
