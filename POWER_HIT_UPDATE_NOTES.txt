LATEST POWER HIT UPDATE

1. Every round now resolves both players' attacks: blue power hits the red fighter and red power hits the blue fighter. Both health bars can decrease in the same exchange.
2. Damage is calculated from each selected power's strength. Shield powers reduce incoming damage; healing powers restore health.
3. The result animation now applies the correct reaction to both fighters independently, including falling when health reaches zero.
4. Power projectiles are larger, brighter, and animate toward the centre of the arena. The central impact flash is stronger so the hit is easier to see.
5. The power-selection controls now show only 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Player 1's buttons are blue; Player 2's buttons are red. Accessible labels retain the underlying power names for assistive technology.
6. The centre collision label is shortened to HIT.
